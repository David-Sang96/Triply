Common questions

How do I plan a trip?

Tap Generate, then tell Triply where you want to go, how many days you have, who is coming, and what you enjoy. It writes a day-by-day plan and checks each place on a map before showing it to you. This usually takes under a minute, and you can leave the screen while it runs — the trip appears under Trips when it is ready.

My trip failed. What now?

A trip can fail if the destination is too vague, or if a service Triply depends on is briefly unavailable. Tap Retry and try again — a failed trip does not use up one of your trip slots.

If the same destination keeps failing, add the country: "Bagan, Myanmar" rather than "Bagan".

It says I've reached the limit of 5 trips

Each account can keep five saved trips. Delete one you have finished with and you can plan another straight away. Trips that failed do not count towards the five.

It says the AI is busy

Triply runs on a free tier of its AI provider, which has an hourly quota. When that quota is used up, generating a trip or sending a message fails for a short while. Wait a minute and try again — nothing is lost.

Can I change a plan after it's made?

Open the trip and use Ask AI about this trip. The assistant knows your whole itinerary, so you can ask it to make a day cheaper, swap an activity, or slow the pace. It suggests the changes and you apply the ones you want — it cannot rewrite the saved plan by itself. You can also replace the cover photo with one of your own.

Why is one of my activities missing from the map?

Every place name is looked up against a public map database. When no match is found, the activity is still kept in your plan but without coordinates, so it has no pin. Treat a missing pin as a hint to check the place yourself before you set out.

How accurate are the times, prices, and opening hours?

Treat them as a starting point. The plan is written by an AI model, so costs are rough estimates for the whole group and opening hours are not checked. Confirm anything time-sensitive — tickets, bookings, entry rules — with the business itself before you rely on it.

Can I sign in with Apple?

Not yet. Today you can sign in with an email address and password, or with your Google account. Apple sign-in is planned.

Where are my language and currency settings?

In Profile → Preferences. They are saved on your device and are used as the starting point for new trips.

How do I delete something?

  • One message — deleting a message in a conversation removes your question and the reply together.
  • A whole conversation — from the assistant screen.
  • A trip — from the trip screen. Its days, activities, and any photo you uploaded for it go with it.
  • Your whole account — Profile → Account → Delete account. This erases your trips, conversations, and uploaded photos. It cannot be undone; see Delete your account for exactly what goes and what does not.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Email luainawl@gmail.com and we will put it together by hand within 30 days. There is no self-service export button yet.

Reporting something serious

For a security problem, or anything involving another person's data, email luainawl@gmail.com and say so in the subject line. Please do not post the details publicly until we have had a chance to fix it.
