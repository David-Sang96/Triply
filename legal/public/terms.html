3. What Triply is

Triply plans trips. You give it a destination, a length, the number of travellers, a budget level, your interests, and a pace. An AI model then writes a day-by-day itinerary: a theme for each day and three activities per day — morning, afternoon, and evening — each with a short description, an estimated cost, and a place that is looked up on a map. A travel assistant is also available for questions, either in general or about a trip you have already generated.

Triply is a planning tool, not a travel agency. We do not sell, arrange, book, or act as agent for flights, accommodation, tours, tickets, transport, or insurance. We are not a party to any arrangement you make with a business a plan suggests, and we take no responsibility for it.

9. AI-generated itineraries and answers — please read

Itineraries and assistant replies are produced by an AI model. That has real limits, and you accept them when you use Triply:

  • The output may be wrong. A model can state something that sounds confident and is not true. It may name a place that has closed, moved, changed its name, or does not exist.
  • Costs, times, and hours are estimates only. An estimated cost is the model's rough guess for the whole group, not a quoted price. Opening hours, travel times, and ticket availability are not checked and change constantly.
  • Map coordinates are a best effort. Each place name is looked up against a public map database. When no match is found, the activity is kept without coordinates, so a pin may be missing or, in rare cases, wrong.
  • No advice. Nothing Triply produces is professional advice — not medical, legal, financial, immigration, visa, or safety advice. It is not a risk assessment of a place, a route, or an activity.
  • Check anything that matters before you rely on it. Confirm opening hours, prices, bookings, entry rules, visa and health requirements, local laws, and local conditions with the business or the relevant authority. Your own judgement, and official travel advice from your government, come first.
  • Output is not unique to you. Another user asking for a similar trip may receive a similar plan. We make no claim that a generated plan is original, and we give you no exclusive rights over it.
  • The assistant cannot change a saved trip. It can suggest changes; you apply them yourself.
  • Generation can fail. A trip may end in a failed state and need to be tried again.

Do not use Triply as your only source of information for a decision that affects your safety, your money, or your legal position.
