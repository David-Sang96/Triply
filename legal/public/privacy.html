Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026 · Applies to the Triply mobile app and this website.

Not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Everything below was written by reading Triply's source code, so it describes what the app actually does rather than what a template says. It should still be checked by a qualified lawyer before Triply is released.

1. Summary

Triply is an AI trip planner. You tell it where you want to go, and it writes a day-by-day itinerary. To do that, it stores your account details, the trips you create, and your conversations with the assistant.

Three things are worth knowing up front:

  • Triply does not sell your personal data, and does not show ads.
  • Your trip details are sent to Google Gemini to write the itinerary. Your name, email address, and account number are not included in that request.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time. Doing so erases your trips, your conversations, and any photo you uploaded.

2. Who is responsible for your data

Triply is published by David Sang, an individual developer. In data protection law that makes him the "data controller" — the person who decides what data is collected and why.

For any privacy question, or to make one of the requests in section 11, email privacy@triply.app.

3. Who may use Triply

Triply is for people aged 18 and over. It is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. See section 12.

4. What we collect

4.1 Account details

You sign in with an email address and password, or with your Google account. Sign-in is handled by Clerk, an authentication service. Clerk stores your password — it never reaches Triply's own servers, and we never see it.

Once your account exists, Triply copies four things from Clerk into its own database:

  • your account number (an identifier issued by Clerk);
  • your primary email address;
  • your first and last name, if your sign-in provider supplied them;
  • the web address of your profile picture, if you have one.

That copy is kept up to date when you change your details, and is deleted when you delete your account.

4.2 Trips you create

When you plan a trip, Triply stores what you asked for and what was generated:

  • your destination, the number of days, the number of travellers, a budget level, your chosen interests, and your preferred pace;
  • the generated title, summary, day themes, and activities — including each place name, its description, an estimated cost, and its map coordinates;
  • the web address of the cover photo and the photographer's credit;
  • the current status of the trip, and a short error message if generation failed.

The map coordinates belong to the places in your itinerary, not to you. Triply never asks for or records your own location — the app requests no location permission at all.

4.3 Your conversations with the assistant

Triply stores every message you send to the assistant and every reply it gives, so the conversation is still there when you come back. Each general conversation is given a short title, taken automatically from the first message you sent in it.

4.4 Photos you upload

You can replace a trip's cover photo with one of your own. If you do, the app asks for permission to read your photo library, shrinks the image on your device, and uploads it to ImageKit, an image hosting service. Triply stores the resulting web address and file identifier so the photo can be shown and later deleted. Only the photo you pick is uploaded — Triply cannot see the rest of your library.

4.5 Crash and performance reports

Triply uses Sentry to find out when the app breaks or runs slowly. Sentry receives:

  • error and crash reports, including the technical stack trace;
  • device and app information the Sentry software collects by default, such as the device model, operating system version, and app version;
  • event logs made of identifiers, counts, and fixed labels — for example which endpoint failed, the HTTP status number, and a category such as network. The wording of an error message is deliberately left out, because that text often repeats what you typed;
  • a short screen recording of the moments before a crash. Recordings mask all text, images, and drawings by default, so the contents of your screen are covered up. Roughly 1 in 100 ordinary sessions is also recorded, to find problems that never produce an error.

One deliberate exception. When you use the assistant, the message you sent and the reply you received are attached to the performance data sent to Sentry. This is the only way to tell why the assistant gave a poor answer. If you would rather not have a conversation recorded this way, do not send it.

In released versions of the app, Sentry is configured not to attach your IP address. Triply also does not tell Sentry which account an event belongs to.

4.6 Kept on your device only

Your language, currency, and default budget choices are saved in your phone's secure storage and are never sent to Triply's servers. They are erased if you delete your account on that device.

Your sign-in token is also held in your phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore).

4.7 What we do not collect

  • No payment details. Triply is free and has no payment system, so there is nothing to collect.
  • No location tracking. The app requests no location permission.
  • No contacts, calendar, microphone, or health data.
  • No advertising or cross-app tracking identifiers. There is no advertising software in the app.
  • No cookies on this website. These pages run no scripts, load nothing from other servers, and set no cookies.

6. Our legal grounds

If the laws of the European Union or the United Kingdom apply to you, our grounds for using your data are:

  • Performing our contract with you — everything needed to run the account and generate the trips you ask for (sections 4.1 to 4.4).
  • Our legitimate interests — keeping the service working, secure, and free of abuse, which covers the diagnostics in section 4.5 and the usage limits in our Terms.
  • Complying with the law — where we must keep or disclose something.

8. How the AI features use your data

This is the part people most often ask about, so it is set out in full.

Generating an itinerary

Only the trip settings are sent to Google Gemini: the destination, the number of days, the number of travellers, the budget level, your interests, and your pace. Your name, email address, and account number are deliberately excluded, so nothing identifying you crosses that boundary.

Talking to the assistant

Your message is sent to Google Gemini, together with up to the last 30 messages in that conversation. If you are asking about a specific trip, a summary of that itinerary is sent as well. The assistant is instructed never to ask you for personal identity details, and asking it to remember such details is not a supported use of Triply.

Please do not type sensitive information into the assistant. Anything you send is stored in your conversation history, sent to Google, and — as explained in section 4.5 — attached to diagnostics in Sentry.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own terms. Triply uses the free tier of the Gemini API, and Google states that prompts sent on its free tiers may be read by people and used to improve its models. Treat anything you type into the assistant as something Google may read.

9. Where your data is held

Triply's providers operate globally, so your data may be stored or processed outside your own country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where transfers out of the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom need a legal safeguard, we rely on the standard contractual terms offered by each provider.

10. How long we keep it

  • Your account, trips, conversations, and photos are kept until you delete them, or until you delete your account. There is no automatic expiry.
  • Deleting your account removes your record and everything attached to it from Triply's database straight away, and deletes uploaded photos from ImageKit. See section 11.
  • Cached map lookups — the coordinates found for a place name — are kept without a time limit so the same place is not looked up twice. These records contain place names only and are not linked to any account.
  • Diagnostics in Sentry and sign-in records in Clerk are deleted automatically at the end of the retention period set for our account with each of those services. We keep no separate copy of either.
  • Support emails are kept for 24 months, then deleted.

11. Your choices and rights

These controls are built into the app and work immediately:

  • Delete one message. Removing a message in a conversation removes both your question and the reply.
  • Delete a whole conversation from the assistant screen.
  • Delete a trip from the trip screen. Its days, activities, and any photo you uploaded for it go with it.
  • Sign out from Profile → Account.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Account. This deletes your sign-in account, then erases your record and everything linked to it — trips, days, activities, all conversations and messages — and deletes uploaded photos from ImageKit. The preferences saved on that device are erased too. It cannot be undone.
  • Change your name, email address, or profile picture through your sign-in provider; Triply's copy is updated automatically.
  • Photo library permission can be withdrawn at any time in your phone's settings. Only the custom cover feature stops working.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to have it erased, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to complain to your data protection authority. There is no self-service export button in the app yet, so email privacy@triply.app and we will handle the request by hand within 30 days. We may need to confirm that you own the account before acting.

12. Children

Triply is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. The app does not currently ask your age, so if you believe a child has created an account, email privacy@triply.app and we will delete it.

13. How your data is protected

  • All traffic between the app and Triply's servers uses HTTPS.
  • Every request that touches your data must carry a valid sign-in token, which is checked on the server before anything is read or written.
  • Requests are scoped to the account in that token, so one account cannot read or delete another's trips or messages.
  • Your sign-in token is stored in your phone's secure storage, not in ordinary app storage.
  • Messages arriving from Clerk are rejected unless their cryptographic signature is valid.
  • Passwords are held by Clerk and never reach Triply's own servers.

No service can promise perfect security. If a breach ever affects your data, we will notify you and any regulator we are required to inform.
