Your data

Delete your account

You can delete your Triply account yourself, in about ten seconds. Deleting the account also deletes everything stored with it. It cannot be undone.

From inside the app

  1. Open Triply and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Find the Account card and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is removed straight away and you are signed out.

By email, if you can't sign in

Send a message to privacy@triply.app from the email address on the account, with Delete my account as the subject. We will confirm it is your account and delete it within 30 days — usually much sooner.

We ask you to write from the account's own address because that is how we check the request is really yours. Without that check, anyone could delete someone else's trips.

What gets deleted

All of this is erased when your account is deleted:

  • your sign-in account, including your email address, name, and profile picture;
  • every trip you generated, with its days, activities, places, and costs;
  • every conversation with the assistant, and every message in them;
  • any cover photo you uploaded, removed from the image service that stored it;
  • the language, currency, and budget preferences saved on your device.

There is no waiting period and no "deactivated" state. The records are deleted, not hidden, and we cannot bring them back afterwards.

What is not deleted, and why

Two things outlive the account. Neither can identify you once it is gone:

  • Crash and performance reports. Triply sends error and performance data to Sentry, which deletes it automatically at the end of its own retention period. These records are not labelled with your account, so there is nothing in them to look you up by.
  • Cached map lookups. When a place name is turned into map coordinates, the result is cached so the same place is never looked up twice. Those rows hold a place name and its coordinates only — no account is attached to them.

Our Privacy Policy explains both in more detail, along with everything Triply stores while your account exists.

Deleting less than everything

You do not have to delete the whole account to clear things out. Inside the app you can delete a single message together with its reply, a whole conversation, or an individual trip along with its days, activities, and uploaded cover photo.
